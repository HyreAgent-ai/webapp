What HyreAgent is

An automated job-search assistant. It finds relevant job postings, tailors your resume and cover letters with AI, and tracks the applications you submit. Right now it's a closed beta — invite-only, capped at about five users, run by one person while the product matures.

What we ask you to agree to

  1. You're an adult and the invite is for you. You're at least 18, and the email/invite was sent to you by Siddardth (not forwarded by a third party).
  2. You won't try to break it. No automated scraping of the app, no attempting to access other users' data, no hammering our endpoints. The closed beta is not a security-research target — if you find something, please email instead of exploiting.
  3. You won't share your account. The invite is for you. Don't post the magic-link URL anywhere.
  4. You'll use it for your own job search. Not someone else's. Not as a service to clients. The 649-contact CRM dataset belongs to one user; HyreAgent isn't a multi-tenant recruiting platform.
  5. You accept that it's a beta. Things will break. Features will change. AI will sometimes hallucinate (so will the cover letters it writes; review them before sending).

What we promise you

  1. Your data is yours. Stored under your user_id, accessible only to you (and to Siddardth, for support). Deletable any time. See the privacy policy for specifics.
  2. We won't sell or share it. Not with marketers, not with recruiters, not with data brokers.
  3. We won't email your contacts. If you import a LinkedIn CSV, those contacts are a private CRM for your reference only. We won't reach out to them on your behalf or theirs.
  4. We won't surprise you with charges. The beta is free. If/when we charge, you'll know weeks in advance, and the beta as you experience it will keep working until the price model is agreed.
  5. We'll listen. DM Siddardth or email siddardth7@gmail.com. Same human, same day, usually within hours.

What we can't promise

The product is beta. Specifically:

  • No uptime SLA. We aim for "mostly works." If you need 99.9% reliability for your job search, we're not yet that product.
  • No accuracy guarantees on AI output. The resume tailoring, the cover letters, the analyzed job fits — all are AI-generated. Review every piece before sending it to a recruiter. We don't have liability for an AI-written sentence that mischaracterizes your experience.
  • No guarantees on third-party scrapers. Job boards change their structure; sometimes our scrapers miss days of data. We work to fix that, but missing a job posting is not grounds for a refund (the beta is free) or any other remedy.
  • No employment promises. HyreAgent helps you apply faster and to better-fit jobs. It does not get you the job. The application itself, the interview, the hiring decision — those are between you and the employer.

How you leave

Settings → "Delete my account." Wipes your data within 24 hours. No exit interview, no email campaign trying to win you back, no dark patterns. The invite link can re-enroll you for 30 days if you change your mind; after that, you'd need a fresh invite.

How we leave

If the closed beta is sunset (because we move to a public launch, or because we decide the product isn't viable, or for any other reason), we'll give you 30 days' notice by email, let you download all your data via Settings → "Download my data," then delete the database.
